Inventory

Your collection, one lot per printing you own: card, set, condition, finish, language and quantity, plus what you paid and when if you want profit tracking.

Add cards one at a time, paste a decklist, or import a Deckbox/CardCastle-style CSV.

Market

Values every lot at its latest TCGPlayer market price (via MTGJSON) and compares that with what you paid, giving cost basis, market value and unrealized PnL. Each card, and the portfolio as a whole, has a price-history chart.

{% if allow_price_update %} Click Update prices on the Market page to download prices. {% else %} Prices refresh automatically once a day. {% endif %}
